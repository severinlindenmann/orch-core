How the agent works

A checklist, and a gate before every next step

Ticket

never rewritten by the agent
agent files it

Requirements & acceptance criteria
you sign

Plan approved

you sign

Branch, code, tests, evidence

Gate 3
Verdict
pass, or send back
you sign
Agents work between the gates and stop at each one. Only a person can open a gate: the signature is checked by orch, with your key, not left to the agent’s goodwill. Questions park the work with a recommended answer until you reply.
