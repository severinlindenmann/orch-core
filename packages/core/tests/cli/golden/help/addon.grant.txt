Grant an addon its capabilities (owner only); also enables it.
usage: orch addon grant NAME [--digest DIGEST] [--caps CAPS] [--staged STAGE] [--dry-run]
  NAME  addon name
  --digest DIGEST  digest
  --caps CAPS  caps
  --staged STAGE  the stage id that `orch addon install` or `update` made (the package comes from it, not from addons/)
  --dry-run  say what would happen, append nothing
more: orch describe addon.grant
