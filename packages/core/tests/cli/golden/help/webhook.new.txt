Make a doorbell address that wakes one follow-up when an outside job calls it.
usage: orch webhook new [REF] --label LABEL [--source github|generic|databricks]
  REF  ticket REF (DEMO-0043, 43); default: your claim
  --label LABEL  what it is for (required)
  --source SOURCE  who will call it, with a hub: github, generic (a script) or databricks (github|generic|databricks)
more: orch describe webhook.new
