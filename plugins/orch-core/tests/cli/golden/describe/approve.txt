ok describe approve
Approve a gate (requirements, plan or code) with your signature.
usage: orch approve requirements|plan|code [--ref REF] [--dry-run]
who: human · emits: gate.approved
pre: ticket_exists gate_current approver_eligible user_presence
args:
  GATE  gate (requirements|plan|code)
  --ref REF  ticket REF (flag)
  --dry-run  say what would happen, append nothing
errors:
  ambiguous_ref (exit 2): name the ticket REF, for example DEMO-0043 | fix: orch status
  not_found (exit 2): orch list
  human_only (exit 3): orch ask or orch wait | fix: orch describe ask
  role.denied (exit 3): ask an owner or maintainer | fix: orch status
  transition.refused (exit 3): orch show --section current_state | fix: orch show
  lock.busy (exit 4 retry): retry in a moment | fix: orch status
  gate.stale (exit 5): orch show
  source.missing (exit 5): restore the branch, then retry | fix: orch status
  members.stale (exit 9 retry): retry; the person re-signs | fix: orch status
out: ok {key} gate.approved {gate} seq={seq} / next: {next}
