ok describe ask
Ask a person; works without a grant (unattended). Then orch wait.
usage: orch ask [TEXT] [--ref REF] [--option 'KEY | TITLE | TEXT'] [--fact 'KEY | LABEL | VALUE'] [--rec KEY] [--allow-other] [--details TEXT] [--details-file PATH] [--text-question] [--question-file PATH] [--to WHO] [--why TEXT] [--non-blocking] [--dry-run]
who: unattended · emits: question.asked
pre: ticket_exists ticket_visible unattended_scope text_clean unattended_quota
args:
  TEXT  the question (up to 140 characters)
  --ref REF  ticket REF (flag); default: your claim
  --option 'KEY | TITLE | TEXT'  an answer: 'key | title | text' (2 to 6, text is optional)
  --fact 'KEY | LABEL | VALUE'  a fact of an option: 'key | Label | value' (1 to 3 per option)
  --rec KEY  the recommended option key
  --allow-other  the person may also type an answer of their own
  --details TEXT  more detail for the person (up to 1500 characters)
  --details-file PATH  read the details from PATH, or - for stdin
  --text-question  an open question: the person types the answer, no options
  --question-file PATH  the whole question as JSON, from PATH or - for stdin (not with TEXT or the flags above)
  --to WHO  person id or role to ask
  --why TEXT  why it matters
  --non-blocking  work can go on while it is open
  --dry-run  say what would happen, append nothing
errors:
  ambiguous_ref (exit 2): name the ticket REF, for example DEMO-0043 | fix: orch status
  not_found (exit 2): orch list
  grant.expired (exit 3): ask the person for a new grant | fix: orch status
  grant.required (exit 3): ask the person to run orch grant, then set ORCH_GRANT | fix: orch status
  lock.busy (exit 4 retry): retry in a moment | fix: orch status
  parse.text (exit 6): orch describe ask
  quota.unattended (exit 9 retry): ask the person for a grant | fix: orch status
out: ok {key} question.asked {question} seq={seq} / next: {next}
