ok describe grant.revoke
Revoke a grant; every session using it ends.
usage: orch grant revoke GRANT [--reason TEXT] [--dry-run]
who: human · emits: grant.revoked
pre: workspace_exists role_allows user_presence text_clean
args:
  GRANT  grant id
  --reason TEXT  why
  --dry-run  say what would happen, append nothing
errors:
  not_found (exit 2): orch list | fix: orch list
  human_only (exit 3): orch ask or orch wait | fix: orch describe ask
  role.denied (exit 3): ask an owner or maintainer | fix: orch status
  lock.busy (exit 4 retry): retry in a moment | fix: orch status
  parse.text (exit 6): orch describe grant.revoke | fix: orch describe grant.revoke
  members.stale (exit 9 retry): retry; the person re-signs | fix: orch status
out: ok grant.revoked {grant} seq={seq} / next: {next}
