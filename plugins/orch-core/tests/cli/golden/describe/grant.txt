ok describe grant
Issue a standing grant for agents; the secret is printed once, on your terminal.
usage: orch grant [--hours N] [--scope all|workable] [--verbs VERBS] [--label TEXT] [--dry-run]
who: human · emits: grant.issued
pre: workspace_exists role_allows user_presence text_clean
args:
  --hours N  length in whole hours (1 to 24) [default 8]
  --scope SCOPE  what it covers (all|workable) [default workable]
  --verbs VERBS  agent, or operation names comma separated [default agent]
  --label TEXT  short label
  --dry-run  say what would happen, append nothing
errors:
  human_only (exit 3): orch ask or orch wait | fix: orch describe ask
  role.denied (exit 3): ask an owner or maintainer | fix: orch status
  lock.busy (exit 4 retry): retry in a moment | fix: orch status
  parse.text (exit 6): orch describe grant
  members.stale (exit 9 retry): retry; the person re-signs | fix: orch status
out: ok grant.issued {grant} until={until} / next: {next}
