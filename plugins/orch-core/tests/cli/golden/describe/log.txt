ok describe log
Add a note to the ticket log; works without a grant (unattended).
usage: orch log TEXT [--ref REF] [--dry-run]
who: unattended · emits: log.added
pre: ticket_exists ticket_visible text_clean unattended_quota
args:
  TEXT  the note, at most 4096 bytes
  --ref REF  ticket REF (flag); default: your claim
  --dry-run  say what would happen, append nothing
errors:
  ambiguous_ref (exit 2): name the ticket REF, for example DEMO-0043 | fix: orch status
  not_found (exit 2): orch list | fix: orch list
  lock.busy (exit 4 retry): retry in a moment | fix: orch status
  parse.text (exit 6): orch describe log | fix: orch describe log
  quota.unattended (exit 9 retry): ask the person for a grant | fix: orch status
out: ok {key} log.added seq={seq} / next: {next}
