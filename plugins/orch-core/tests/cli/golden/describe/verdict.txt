ok describe verdict
Give the verify verdict: pass, or fail with a reason.
usage: orch verdict pass|fail [--message TEXT] [--ref REF] [--dry-run]
who: human · emits: verdict.given
pre: ticket_exists ticket_in_testing gate_current approver_eligible user_presence text_clean
args:
  OUTCOME  outcome (pass|fail)
  -m, --message TEXT  free text
  --ref REF  ticket REF (flag)
  --dry-run  say what would happen, append nothing
errors:
  not_found (exit 2): orch list | fix: orch list
  human_only (exit 3): orch ask or orch wait | fix: orch describe ask
  role.denied (exit 3): ask an owner or maintainer | fix: orch status
  transition.refused (exit 3): orch show --section current_state | fix: orch show
  lock.busy (exit 4 retry): retry in a moment | fix: orch status
  gate.stale (exit 5): orch show | fix: orch show
  source.missing (exit 5): restore the branch, then retry | fix: orch status
  parse.text (exit 6): orch describe verdict | fix: orch describe verdict
  members.stale (exit 9 retry): retry; the person re-signs | fix: orch status
out: ok {key} verdict.given {outcome} seq={seq} / next: {next}
