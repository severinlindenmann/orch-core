ok describe wait
Wait for an answer, an approval, a change request, a verdict or an invalidation (default 540 s).
usage: orch wait [--ref REF] [--timeout SECONDS] [--strict-timeout]
who: read · emits: -
pre: ticket_exists ticket_visible
args:
  --ref REF  ticket REF (flag); default: your claim
  --timeout SECONDS  seconds to wait [default 540]
  --strict-timeout  exit 7 when the timeout passes
errors:

  not_found (exit 2): orch list | fix: orch list
  wait.timeout (exit 7 retry): orch wait | fix: orch wait
out: ok {key} wait {kind} cursor={cursor} / next: {next}
