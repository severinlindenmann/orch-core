Claim a ticket, the next one, or take over another session's claim.
usage: orch claim [REF] [--next] [--takeover] [--reason TEXT] [--dry-run]
  REF  ticket REF; default: the next ticket
  --next  claim the next ticket
  --takeover  take the claim from another session (needs --reason)
  --reason TEXT  why you take over
  --dry-run  say what would happen, append nothing
more: orch describe claim
