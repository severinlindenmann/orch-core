Issue a standing grant for agents; the secret is printed once, on your terminal.
usage: orch grant [--hours N] [--days N] [--scope all|workable] [--verbs VERBS] [--label TEXT] [--extend GRANT] [--runner] [--dry-run]
  --hours N  length in whole hours (1 to 168)
  --days N  length in whole days (1 to 7); instead of --hours
  --scope SCOPE  what it covers (all|workable) [default workable]
  --verbs VERBS  agent, or operation names comma separated [default agent]
  --label TEXT  short label
  --extend GRANT  revoke this runner grant of yours together with the new one (with --runner)
  --runner  hand the secret to the runner here instead of showing it (at most the grant_hours setting)
  --dry-run  say what would happen, append nothing
more: orch describe grant
